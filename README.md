# 2048 PANTONE

A fan-made colour-chart edition of the 2048 sliding-tile puzzle. Eleven PANTONE swatches, from cool grey at 2 to pinky at 2048.

Una edición de carta de color: once muestras PANTONE, del gris frío en el 2 al rosa en el 2048.

**Play:** [English](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-PANTONE/) · [Español](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-PANTONE/es/)

## How to play

Arrow keys move the whole board; a swipe does the same on a phone. Two tiles of the same value merge into the next one on the ladder, and the value of the new tile is added to your score. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match, so leave a corner free and plan one merge ahead.
