How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles of the same colour touch, they become the next colour!
Hit 'Z' to take back one or more moves.

2048 PANTONE works as a colour chart you can shuffle. Eleven swatches run from a cool grey at 2 through turquoise, brown, magenta, yellow, cyan, green, orange, lime and navy to pinky at 2048, and the number on each swatch is also its score value. Push the 4×4 grid with the arrow keys or a swipe; two swatches of the same shade become the next colour in the sequence. Z undoes a move, which is worth remembering when the grid is one square from full. The palette tops out at pinky, and that tile is the one to chase.
